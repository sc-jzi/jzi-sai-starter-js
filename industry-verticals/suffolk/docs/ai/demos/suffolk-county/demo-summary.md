# Demo summary — Suffolk County Government

## What was built

- Brand theme (`site-suffolk`): navy `#003366`, civic blue `#006FE6`, alert red `#D2232A`, Heebo + Arimo + BenchNine.
- Home datasources under Data/Promos, all named `Suffolk County - …`.
- **Promo carousel** with six slides:
  1. Emergency Preparedness Tips for Families
  2. Suffolk Stop Bullying
  3. Suffolk County Police Reform and Reinvention Task Force
  4. Invest in your personal safety and protect your property - Hazard Mitigation
  5. SuffolkAlert Emergency Notification System
