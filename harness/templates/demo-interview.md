# Demo interview (no transcript)

Use when there is no discovery transcript. Ask in rounds of at most 4 questions; skip anything the SE already told you. Free text is fine. Mark every answer *stated by the SE*; unknown stays a gap.
If the SE has a colleague's discovery or demo-flow prompt, use its structure for the brief and only ask what it does not cover.

## Round 1 — who and why
1. Who is the prospect (name, website URL) and who is in the audience (roles, decision maker, champion)?
2. What triggered the conversation: the pain or event, in the prospect's own words if known?
3. What is the desired outcome of the demo (the next step the SE wants)?
4. How long is the demo and is it live in front of the prospect or recorded?

## Round 2 — pains and moments (Chain of Pain)
1. The top 3 pains, ranked: for each who feels it, why it matters, and any number (cost, time, volume) the prospect stated.
2. What would be the "last thing first": the result the audience should see in the first minute?
3. Which Sitecore capabilities must be shown (and which must not)?
4. Competitors or the current platform in play?

## Round 3 — content and look
1. Pages or journeys the SE already has in mind (home, product, article, landing, search ...)?
2. Which real pages of the prospect's site should be mirrored (URLs), which are composed from the library?
3. Any brand constraints (colours, fonts, tone, language other than English)?
4. Collection: reuse an existing one (list with `sites-api.mjs collections`) or create a new one named after the customer?

Output: the same `demo-brief.md` as the transcript path, with an "Inputs" line saying it came from an SE interview.
